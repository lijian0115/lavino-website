About Us

Qingdao Lavino Equipment & Parts Co., Ltd. is a China-based supplier of agricultural machinery and spare parts, serving distributors, dealers, and importers worldwide.

Our products are mainly focused on two categories:

1. Agricultural Machinery & Spare Parts

We supply rotary tillers, seeders, harvesters, mowers, vegetable transplanters, self-propelled sprayers, and other agricultural machinery, together with related spare and wear parts for convenient and efficient sourcing.

2. Replacement Filters

We supply hydraulic filters, fuel filters, oil filters, air filters, and other replacement filters for agricultural machinery, construction machinery, heavy-duty equipment, trucks, and other vehicles, covering applications for many well-known Chinese and international brands.

In addition to our standard product range, we provide OEM and customized services to meet different customer and market requirements.

Located in Shandong Province with convenient access to Qingdao Port, Lavino benefits from a strong local supply chain and efficient logistics network, helping customers consolidate products, arrange faster shipments, and achieve competitive logistics costs.

From complete agricultural machinery to filters and spare parts, Lavino is committed to providing flexible and efficient one-stop sourcing solutions for customers worldwide.
