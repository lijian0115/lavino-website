Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

1. Who we are

This website (www.lavinoequipment.com) is operated by Qingdao Lavino Equipment & Parts Co., Ltd. ("Lavino", "we", "us"), a supplier of agricultural machinery, spare parts and replacement filters based in Qingdao, Shandong, China. We are the controller of the personal information collected through this website.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, send us an email, or contact us on WhatsApp, we may collect your name, company name, email address, country, telephone number and the details of your request.

Information collected automatically. When you browse this website, we and our service providers may automatically collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type and version, device type, the pages you visit and the time of your visit.

3. How we use your information

  • to respond to your enquiry and prepare quotations;
  • to communicate with you about products, orders and after-sales service;
  • to improve the content, performance and usability of this website;
  • to meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies. Strictly necessary cookies keep the site working — for example, remembering your language preference. If analytics is enabled, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site; Google may set cookies and process usage data on our behalf. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, and the website will still work.

5. Service providers and sharing

We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate this website, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Form processing — enquiry forms are delivered to our email inbox by a third-party form service;
  • Hosting and content delivery — this website is hosted on cloud infrastructure with a global content delivery network;
  • Analytics — Google Analytics 4, if enabled.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, such transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep information

Enquiries and business correspondence are kept for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, accounting and tax requirements, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

7. Security

This website is served over HTTPS. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, to request data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. If you are in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. If you are a California resident, you have the right to know, to delete, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — we do not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

This website is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
